About me

More than a developer — someone who understands business too

I'm Khayelisha Nick Stevens, a final-year Diploma in Information Technology student at Vaal University of Technology. I've always been interested in technology, but over the past few years my interests have grown well beyond programming.

I enjoy building websites, systems and digital solutions — but I've also developed a real interest in marketing, sales, business development and understanding what actually makes customers choose one business over another. I've worked on more than six systems and projects so far, some built independently and others developed as part of a team, and I've been involved in launching real systems that organisations use today.

What I enjoy most is talking to people and understanding their problems. I don't think technology should exist just because it's technically impressive — it should solve a real problem and create value for the person or business using it. That's also why I enjoy helping other people and businesses turn a rough idea into something practical they can actually use.

Faith & values

I'm a child of God, and my faith is a real part of who I am — I was saved in 2024. It shapes how I try to treat people and how I think about business, grounded in a simple principle:

"Love the Lord your God with all your heart, soul and mind" — and "love your neighbour as yourself." (Matthew 22:37–39)

So instead of only asking what business I can build for myself, I try to also ask: how can I use what I know to help someone else?
